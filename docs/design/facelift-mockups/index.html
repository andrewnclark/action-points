ActionPoints facelift — design directions

Takes on the restrained dev-tool genre, each mocked as a full homepage and a full Review screen. The headline, lede, three steps, pricing facts and the six Action Points are held the same across every direction, so the comparison is about look and feel rather than wording; only small per-direction chrome (a keyboard hint, a word count) differs. The winner gets rebuilt as real design tokens.

What to look at

  • Homepage: does it read as a finished commercial product, or as a template? Would you paste a real transcript into it?
  • Review: can you curate quickly — is it obvious what accept, reject, and edit do, and which card is being edited?
  • Each Review shows the same six Action Points from the sample product-sync transcript, with one rejected and one open in the editor, so the states are comparable.
  • Every font is SIL Open Font License and self-hosted from fonts/ — no CDN, nothing to license.
  • These are throwaway artifacts. The winner is rebuilt as semantic design tokens in the app (issue #15); the losing directions are not maintained.
